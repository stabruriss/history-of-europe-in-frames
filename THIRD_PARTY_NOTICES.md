## Natural Earth

The coastlines, lakes, rivers and modern borders in `src/data.json` and `src/history_geo.json` are derived from Natural Earth 1:10m and 1:50m data, which is in the public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

## Esri terrain tiles

When the page is online, it loads the Esri World Physical Map and World Shaded Relief tiles from `server.arcgisonline.com` (© Esri, US National Park Service). These tiles are not included in this repository. Their use is subject to Esri's terms.

## Quoted texts

Scripture quotations are taken from the Chinese Union Version (和合本, 1919), which is in the public domain. Quotations from Chinese histories such as the 晋书 are in the public domain. Other classical sources are quoted in short translations made for this atlas. Reading links point to external sites, and each of those sites has its own terms.
